Data deletion instructions

Last updated: November 2, 2026
App: UsPodsach2D · Meta App ID: 1684965449634727
Operator: EagleGlobal

These instructions explain how an authorized Page or business operator can stop a connection and request removal of information stored by UsPodsach2D.

1. Disconnect local access, if you operate the app

In the local Page Manager, open Settings → Kết nối token (token connections), select the relevant connection and use its disconnect action. This removes that connection’s locally stored Meta and derived Page tokens and disables its linked access. Jobs that depend on the old connection cannot continue using that credential.

Disconnecting does not erase the Page catalog, cached posts, schedules, local media or audit history. If other active connections are authorized for the same Page, they are separate connections. Review those as needed. To revoke access on Meta itself, the business administrator must also review the relevant app, system user and asset permissions in Meta Business Settings or the relevant Meta account integration settings.

2. Request deletion of stored information

  1. Email nguyenthanhnhatanh8386@gmail.com with the subject UsPodsach2D data deletion request.
  2. State which Page, business or app connection the request concerns. Include a Page name or Page ID and the connection label if known, and describe what you want removed. Include a reply address where the operator can contact you.
  3. EagleGlobal will review the request and may ask for reasonable verification that you are authorized to manage the relevant Page, business or connection before making changes.
  4. After verification, the operator will coordinate manual removal of the applicable local records and files, review related queued jobs so they do not recreate the removed information, and reply with the outcome or any limitation that prevents completion.
Do not send secrets. Do not email access tokens, passwords, two-factor authentication codes or recovery codes. Do not send identity documents with an initial request.

3. What deletion covers

Depending on the request, local information can include connection credentials, cached Page and post information, associated workflow records, schedules, performance records, and uploaded or generated media. The operator must review dependencies and other authorized connections before removing shared records.

Removing local records does not delete a Facebook Page, Reel, post or image already held by Meta. The Page administrator must manage published Facebook content through Facebook or an authorized Meta tool. Removal of a shop product, content held by a production provider, or copies outside this application requires a separate request or action with the relevant service or storage owner.

UsPodsach2D currently uses these contact-based instructions. It does not provide an automated Meta data-deletion callback or promise an automatic purge merely because a connection is disconnected.

4. Questions

Contact EagleGlobal at nguyenthanhnhatanh8386@gmail.com. More information about data use is in the privacy policy.
