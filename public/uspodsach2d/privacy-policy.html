1. Scope and purpose

UsPodsach2D is an internal Facebook Page management tool for EagleGlobal’s own business portfolio. Authorized operators use it to read Page and published-content information, edit Page details and images, prepare media, and schedule or publish Reels using permissions granted through Meta.

This policy covers information processed by UsPodsach2D. Meta, email providers, and optional content-production or shop services have their own privacy policies for their services. This policy does not replace those policies or the privacy policy for purchases from a shop.

2. Information the app processes

  • Connections and authorization: access tokens, connection labels, app and business portfolio IDs, connected identity IDs and names, available permissions, connection status and relevant timestamps.
  • Page information: Page IDs, names, links, categories, bio or about text, website, business phone or address when available, opening information, follower counts and Page images.
  • Published content: post and video IDs, post text, publishing times, links, publishing status and available aggregate video view counts. The app’s current token-management features do not collect individual viewers’ identities, private inbox messages or comments.
  • Operator-provided content: artwork, product images, videos, captions, product links, file names and paths, file sizes, media types, integrity hashes, and content-production instructions or review results.
  • Schedules and operational records: the selected Page and connection, scheduled time and time zone, job status, resulting video links, error summaries, edit request records and workflow audit information.
  • Support requests: information an individual chooses to send by email, including contact details and information needed to identify and resolve the request.

Access is limited by the token permissions and Page assets authorized in Meta. The Page Manager token service does not import Chrome Manager browser profiles, proxy credentials, Outlook mailboxes or its separate publishing queue.

3. How information is used

Information is used to verify the intended Page and connection, display and synchronize the Page catalog, make operator-authorized Page updates, upload and publish selected media, run saved schedules, verify publishing results, avoid duplicate uploads or jobs, troubleshoot failures, and compare aggregate video performance. Support information is used to handle questions and data requests.

Optional idea-production workflows run when an operator starts them. They prepare product imagery and videos and associate the content with its verified product link. Discovering a file alone does not start an idea-production run.

4. Storage and protection

The operational application runs on the operator’s Windows computer. Page information, schedules and workflow records are stored in its local application database; source and generated media are stored in local files. This is separate from the public policy website.

Stored Meta access tokens and derived Page tokens are protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), bound to the Windows account. The local web interface does not return those tokens to its frontend. Meta API requests use HTTPS with authorization headers. Local application access uses local-host checks, session cookies and request protections. These measures reduce risk but are not a guarantee that information can never be accessed improperly.

5. Services that receive information

  • Meta: receives access credentials needed for authorized API calls, Page changes, selected media and captions, and publishing requests. Content published to Facebook is subject to Facebook’s audience and platform settings.
  • Optional production and shop tools: operator-started content workflows may send selected artwork, quotes, images, production instructions or product information to the configured Codex/OpenAI production tools and PODSach/Blueox shop workflow. The production worker is not given permission to publish to Facebook through this workflow.
  • Website and email providers: a hosting provider may process ordinary network request information to deliver these policy pages, and the email provider processes messages sent to the contact address below.

These policy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking pixels or forms.

6. Retention

Local operational records and media are retained while needed for Page administration, publishing history, duplicate prevention, troubleshooting or handling requests. The app does not apply a general automatic deletion period to all records or media; removal requires action by the operator. Its activity list is capped at the latest 500 events, and Page avatar images are cached in memory for up to 15 minutes.

Disconnecting a connection removes that connection’s stored tokens and disables its linked access. It does not automatically erase cached Page information, posts, schedules, local media or historical records. It also does not revoke every authorization in Meta or delete content already published on Facebook.

7. Data requests and deletion

You may contact EagleGlobal to ask about information associated with an authorized Page or connection, request correction, or request deletion of information stored by the app. See the data deletion instructions for the steps and the distinction between local records and content held by Meta.

The operator may need to verify your authority over the relevant Page, business or connection before acting. Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes or identity documents with an initial request.
